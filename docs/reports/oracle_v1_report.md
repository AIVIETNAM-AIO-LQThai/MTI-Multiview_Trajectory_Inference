## 3. Primary cell: L=8, q_w=0.5, λ=0.1, η=0.05, β=0.2, uniform π (N=100,000)

| quantity | estimate ± 95% half-width |
|---|---|
| **V_2** (aware vs naive) | **0.0712 ± 0.0031** (4.3% of V_2) |
| **I_loss** (irrecoverable) | **0.2614 ± 0.0069** |
| X_naive (naive excess over clean-prefix oracle) | 0.3309 ± 0.0035 (V_2 + I_loss = 0.3326; consistent) |
| VOI_clean = E[κ μ_H²] (value of clean history over the zero action) | 4.007 ± 0.039 |

Expected one-step costs (analytic given the sampled law; simulated-cost check |z| ≤ 0.6 for all five actions):
zero action 6.931 ± 0.057; mode oracle (unattainable) 1.085 ± 0.005; **clean oracle 2.924 ± 0.022; naive 3.255 ± 0.025; aware 3.186 ± 0.025**.

Reading: the aware oracle removes about 21% of the naive excess (0.069 of 0.331), i.e. **1.8% of the clean history's decision value and 2.1% of the naive
cost**; 79% of the naive excess is information lost to the channel and cannot be recovered by any observer of S.

**Clean vs corrupted records.** On uncorrupted records the aware belief *costs* 0.0497 ± 0.0034 over the clean oracle (it hedges) — the naive action is optimal
there — realised gain D = −0.047 ± 0.008. On corrupted records naive excess is 1.655 ± 0.017, aware 1.112 ± 0.012, gain D = +0.544 ± 0.010. The aware oracle
trades a small loss on the 80% clean records for a gain on the 20% corrupted records; it optimises the mixture risk, not each subgroup.

**Lag strata** (loss when the flip is at lag ℓ = L−1−j; lag 0 = last recorded action): V_2 = 0.485, 0.370, 0.156, 0.078, 0.048, 0.039, 0.037, 0.042 for lags 0…7;
naive excess falls from 8.59 (lag 0) to 0.005 (lag 7). Awareness is valuable only for recent flips; for lags ≥ 4–5 the realised gain D is slightly *negative*
at lags 5–7 (−0.023…−0.040; lag 4 is indistinguishable from 0) — old flips barely hurt the naive filter while the hedge still costs.

**Natural-switch diagnostic** (hidden path, edge m_{L−2}→m_{L−1}, 5.0% of prefixes): V_2 = 0.515 ± 0.050 but realised gain **D = −0.557 ± 0.103** — after a natural switch
into the last recorded mode the aware oracle is *worse* than the naive action (a lag-0 natural switch looks like a lag-0 flip, and the aware belief hedges against it).
Decision-edge switches (m_{L−1}→m_L, unforecastable from any record): D = +0.371 ± 0.074. No switch at all (66.4%): D = +0.170 ± 0.004.
(Hidden-mode strata use κ[(μ̃−m_L)²−(μ_2−m_L)²]; spec §8 A4.)

**Exact corruption posterior** (oracle pipeline check, not a detector): categorical log score 0.683 vs 0.916 nats for the prior-only predictor; location top-1 accuracy
given corruption 0.549 vs 0.125; any-corruption Brier 0.134 vs 0.160. Reliability bins are calibrated (|z| ≤ 1.25, 12 bins). On average the flip
location is substantially identifiable (0.55 vs 0.125), yet the aware oracle recovers only 1.8% of VOI_clean. The lag strata show why the decision value is
small: a flip costs the naive filter little unless it sits at lags 0–2. *(Review note: whether recent flips are also the best-localised ones was not measured — no
top-1-by-lag table — so no claim is made about that.)*

## 4. Oracle map (η=0.05, uniform π; all cells reported; N=100,000 each)

| L | q_w | β | V_2 ± hw | I_loss | X_naive | VOI_clean | V_2/VOI | V_2 hw/V_2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0.5 | 0.2 (primary) | 0.0712 ± 0.0031 | 0.261 | 0.331 | 4.007 | 1.8% | 4.3% |
| 8 | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.3054 ± 0.0074 | 0.515 | 0.820 | 3.983 | 7.7% | 2.4% |
| 8 | 0.02 | 0.2 | 0.0905 ± 0.0028 | 0.207 | 0.303 | 3.118 | 2.9% | 3.0% |
| 8 | 0.02 | 0.5 | **0.4161 ± 0.0070** | 0.344 | 0.761 | 3.132 | **13.3%** | 1.7% |
| 32 | 0.5 | 0.2 | 0.0061 ± 0.0004 | 0.075 | 0.082 | 3.989 | 0.15% | 6.8%† |
| 32 | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.0321 ± 0.0015 | 0.170 | 0.206 | 3.991 | 0.8% | 4.7%† |
| 32 | 0.02 | 0.2 | 0.0081 ± 0.0004 | 0.069 | 0.076 | 3.131 | 0.26% | 4.7%† |
| 32 | 0.02 | 0.5 | 0.0503 ± 0.0018 | 0.141 | 0.190 | 3.131 | 1.6% | 3.5% |

† Sized by the predeclared absolute rule (V̂_2 < 1% of VOI_clean ⇒ half-width ≤ 5×10⁻⁴·VOI_clean ≈ 0.002), which all three meet; only L=32,q_w=0.5,β=0.2 has a relative half-width above 5% (6.8%).
Controls: β=0 and η=½ give V_2=I_loss=X_naive=0 exactly (sums of non-negative per-prefix values are exactly 0; N=20,000 each; pipeline checks, not measurements).

Pattern: V_2 grows with β and with lower observation noise, and shrinks roughly ten-fold from L=8 to L=32. *Review qualification:* the L comparison is confounded by the channel design — β is the
probability of *one* flip anywhere and π is uniform, so the per-position flip rate is β/L and the chance that the flip lands at a harmful recent lag falls with L. The map
therefore does **not** show that longer histories protect against corruption at a fixed per-position rate; that would need a separate cell (e.g. π concentrated on recent lags,
or fixed β/L). I_loss exceeds V_2 in seven of eight cells; the exception is L=8,q_w=.02,β=.5 (V_2 0.416 > I_loss 0.344). V_2/X_naive ranges from 7.4% (L=32,q_w=.5,β=.2) to 54.7% (L=8,q_w=.02,β=.5). The natural-switch
gain D has a negative point estimate in all eight cells (−0.04 … −2.40; −2.40 ± 0.15 at L=8,q_w=.02,β=.5) and is significantly negative (95% interval excludes 0) in seven; the exception is L=32,q_w=.5,β=.2 (−0.042 ± 0.036 is borderline).

## 5. Restricted-summary recalibration, B=(s_L, μ̃) — privileged diagnostic

Fitted on independent prefixes (seed 4001, N=2×10⁴ and 2×10⁵), resolution chosen on independent validation prefixes (seed 5001), evaluated on the main sample; A+R+X = V_2 exactly.
Two function classes (see §7 for why there are two). Rigorous reading for any fixed g: R upper-bounds the exact additional-context term E[κ(μ_B−μ_2)²]; V_2−R lower-bounds the exact
recalibration term E[κ(μ̃−μ_B)²].

- **Residual class** (g = μ̃ + binned correction; contains the identity): attained gain (V_2−R)/V_2 = **8.1%** in the primary cell (A=0.0056, R=0.0654±0.0027, cross term X=+0.0002±0.0004), 7.7%, 10.8%, 11.3% at
  L=8 and 5.7%, 5.8%, 5.8%, 6.8% at L=32. Estimates are insensitive to resolution (6×6…24×24) and fit size (2×10⁴ vs 2×10⁵).
- **Direct class** (g = binned μ_2): 5.3% in the primary cell but **worse than the naive belief** in four of the L=32 cells (−50.7%, −4.6%, −30.4%, −0.6%): bin discretisation error exceeds the small signal. Those rows are an approximation failure, not information.
- Interpretation: at least 6–11% of V_2 is recoverable from (s_L, μ̃) alone; the remaining error R is 89–94% of V_2. Because R only *upper*-bounds the context term, this does **not** prove that the other ~90% needs
  explicit localisation; it shows a two-feature recalibrator that is stable across resolutions captures little more.

## 6. Input-contamination profile (primary cell, first 20,000 prefixes; clean-model transition log-likelihood; thresholds = clean 1%/5% quantiles)

| query type | mean Δll vs ll(H) | frac below clean 1% / 5% quantile |
|---|---|---|
| clean record H (reference) | 0 | 0.010 / 0.049 |
| attacked record S | −1.35 ± 0.02 | 0.043 / 0.157 |
| repair at the true location (= H exactly) | 0 | 0.010 / 0.049 |
| repair at a wrong location (two flips) | −2.34 ± 0.03 | 0.093 / 0.260 |
| folded O_θ(S) (= O_θ(H)) | −0.23 ± 0.01 (sign-marginalisation cost, same as clean folded) | 0.009 / 0.049 |
| folded O_k(S), k≠θ (retains the flip) | −1.49 ± 0.02 | 0.039 / 0.149 |

A single flipped record is mostly inside the clean-model support (84% above the clean 5% quantile): **a changed token does not by itself put a query out of support**. Wrong-location candidate
repairs (two flips) are the rarest queries. Oracle route weights, expected number of contaminated action positions per route: attacked records 0.97 (candidate) vs 0.80 (folded); clean records
0.17 (candidate) vs 0.00 (folded). So the candidate route places more weight on contaminated queries, but **both routes are algebraically identical**: this profile is a proposed *explanation* for
possible finite-sample differences, not evidence of a masked-route advantage.

## 7. Discrepancies, deviations and unresolved assumptions

1. **Post-hoc addition of the residual recalibrator class.** The first full run (commit `bfe9a9d`, direct class only) showed the direct class worse than the naive belief at L=32. I added the residual class (commit
   `0f1b6d1`), chose within-class variants on validation data only, and kept both classes in all tables. Main-sample V_2/I_loss/X etc. were unaffected (fixed seeds; the rerun reproduced them).
2. **Sizing:** all cells ran at the `min_main` floor of 100,000 prefixes (≥ what the rule asked). Three L=32 cells were sized by the absolute rule and one of them has a relative half-width above 5% (6.8%, table §4). Not rerun to avoid changing a predeclared rule after seeing results.
3. D (hidden-mode paired gain) is high-variance; its main-vs-check z was 2.49 in the primary cell (below the |z|≤4 criterion, the largest in the study). D is a diagnostic; V_2 itself uses exact beliefs.
4. T9's argmin check is limited by the scalar optimiser (6e-9), not by the identity (checked to 1e-10 elsewhere). The tolerance for that one check is 1e-6.
5. The virtual environment resolved to Python 3.14.7 (not the system 3.12.10); all tests and results were produced under 3.14.7.
6. Assumptions: exogenous channel (θ independent of modes and probe given H); the aware belief equals E[m_L|S] only under that. Single-flip channel only. All conclusions are specific to this simulator and the fixed one-step action rule.
7. No numerical failures: all log probabilities finite; no clipping or flooring anywhere in the oracle path.

## 9. Implications for scope and the next experiment

- Decision headroom from awareness in this simulator is small in the primary cell (1.8% of the clean history's value; 21% of naive excess) and negligible at L=32. A learned pilot in the primary cell would struggle to show differences
  of practical size: the provisional Δ_min = max(0.10·V_2, 0.002·VOI_clean) = max(0.0071, 0.0080) = **0.0080 — the absolute floor binds (≈11% of V_2)**.
- A more discriminating learned-pilot cell is **L=8, q_w=0.02, β=0.5** (V_2 = 0.416, 13% of VOI_clean; Δ_min = 0.042) — recommended as the second (or lead) cell; L=32 is not worth the learned-arm cost for decision value.
- Natural-switch states are a real cost of awareness (D<0) and must be part of the learned-model diagnostics.

## 10. Opus evidence review (2026-10-04, `claude-opus-5-5`)

Reviewed: this report, the saved JSON (spot-checked every map-cell number above against `results/oracle_v1/*.json`), test maxima, mutation audit,
and the estimator/strata code paths. Verdict: **the exact reference and the first information measurement are sound and may be cited within the declared setting.**

Supported (within S2, the exogenous single-flip channel, the one-step rule):
- C1 exact-route equivalence (analytic and numerically verified to ≤1.5e-14) and C2 (X = V_2 + I_loss).
- C3/C4: V_2 is small in the primary cell (1.8% of VOI_clean; 21% of the naive excess) and material only at low noise with high β at L=8 (13%).
  I_loss ≥ V_2 in 7/8 cells: in most regimes, most of what corruption costs cannot be recovered by *any* observer of the record.
- C10: the aware oracle's mixture-risk gain is paid for on clean records and in natural-switch states. An extra exact check confirms the stratum logic:
  E[D | θ=none] = −E[κ(μ_2−μ_H)² | θ=none] analytically, and the two agree in all 8 cells (e.g. −0.0469 vs −0.0497 primary).
- C5 only as a lower bound (≥6–11% of V_2 from (s_L, μ̃)); C6 only as a descriptive oracle-weight statement.

Qualifications added on review: the L=8 vs L=32 contrast is confounded by the per-position flip rate β/L (§4); the localisation sentence in §3 was reworded
because top-1 accuracy by lag was not measured; the post-hoc residual recalibrator class is acceptable because selection used validation data only, both classes are
reported, and the conclusion (a lower bound) does not depend on the choice.

Not supported / still open: any learned-route difference (H2), the flexibility trade-off (H3), that localisation is necessary, external validity.

**Decision on the next experiment: the learned pilot is justified, but not in the primary physics as lead.** The exact naive filter already has endpoint
𝓔 = V_2, so a learned aware estimator is useful only if it beats V_2. A practically meaningful gap of 10% of V_2 is 0.007 in the primary cell, which is below the absolute floor;
in the q_w=0.02, L=8 physics it is 0.009 (β=0.2) and 0.042 (β=0.5). Revised design is in [learned_pilot_protocol.md](../learned_pilot_protocol.md) (v2).
